FTMO has finally entered the 1-Step market. On paper, it looks perfect: No time limits, 90% profit share, and an End-of-Day (EOD) trailing drawdown. However, the most important number in the PDF isn't the 90% reward—it's the **3% Maximum Daily Loss.**

### The 3% Ceiling vs. The 10% Floor

In the standard FTMO challenge, you have a 5% daily cushion. In the 1-Step, that cushion is reduced to 3%. Mathematically, this creates a much higher **Sequence Risk.** 

If you risk 1% per trade, you are now only **3 consecutive losses** away from losing the account in a single day. In our simulations, a strategy with a 50% win rate has a **22% higher chance** of failing the 1-Step than the 2-Step, specifically because of this tighter daily guardrail.

### Optimization Strategy: The "0.5% Anchor"
Because of the 3% daily limit, our quant engine suggests a maximum risk of **0.5% per trade.** This gives you 6 "units of life" per day, reducing your daily ruin probability to under 2.5%. 

### The Best Day Rule
FTMO 1-Step requires that no single day exceeds 50% of your profit. This means you cannot "gamble" on a single news event to pass. You must prove consistency over multiple sessions.
